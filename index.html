The studio

Contemporary cupping, without the mystique.

Cupping is a traditional practice with a long history across many cultures. At Revivie we offer it in a contemporary setting, for one straightforward reason: our clients tell us sessions leave them feeling looser and more relaxed — and that's exactly how we talk about it. No grand promises, no pseudo-clinical jargon.

Every session starts with a short conversation about how you're moving and feeling, and every cup that touches skin is sterile and single-use or freshly sanitized to spec.

The honest part

Cupping is a traditional practice, not a medical treatment, and it is not a substitute for medical care. We don't claim it treats or cures any condition.

If you're pregnant, take blood-thinning medication, have a skin or clotting condition, or are managing an injury, check with your doctor first — and tell us at booking so we can plan around it or advise you to skip the session.

Session menu

Built as a menu, booked as a conversation.

Classic dry cupping

45 min

Stationary cups across the back, shoulders, or legs. Suction is set to your tolerance and adjusted as you settle. The session most first-timers start with.

Flow (glide) cupping

50 min

Cups moved over oiled skin in long, slow passes — closer to a deep massage in feel. Popular with runners and lifters for the big muscle groups.

Targeted recovery

30 min

A focused session on one area that's been doing too much — neck and shoulders, lower back, calves. Short, specific, easy to fit into a lunch hour.

Revivie full reset

75 min

Dry and glide work combined, head of the back to the calves, with warm towels and a slow cool-down. The unhurried version.

The space

Designed to lower your shoulders the moment you walk in.

One client at a time per room. Warm light, quiet music you'll barely notice, and nothing that smells like a locker room. The studio is being fitted out now — these are the standards it's built to:

  • Private rooms with proper doors — not curtains.
  • Fresh linens every session, cups sanitized to a written protocol you can read on request.
  • No upselling on the table. The quiet room stays quiet.
  • Ten minutes of post-session rest built into every booking — never sold as an add-on.
